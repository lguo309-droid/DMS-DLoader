ASHFALL CITY

Multiplayer PvP looting: raid the ruined skyscrapers for chests (gold blocks), craft weapons, fortify a base and eliminate other survivors. Only 10–15 chests exist at a time (mostly inside buildings) and they relocate with fresh loot every 2 minutes. Click a chest to open it and pick your loot. Find 2 fuel cans and a keycard, reach the rooftop of the Helix Tower (the tall white and orange tower) when the evac helicopter lands, and press F to escape first. A player who dies drops a loot chest with their gear.

WASD move · Space jump · Shift sprint
Left click break / attack / loot · Right click place
1-7 or wheel: select block · E inventory & crafting · H eat · G medkit · F board helicopter
Craft a Workbench, then use it to build a Forge, Chem Table and Radio Bench. Stand near a station to unlock its recipes. Find blueprints in chests to unlock better weapons, a shield and mining tools (Scrap, Reinforced and Military tiers). Hold left click to break blocks (takes time); build spike barricades, alarm wires and shock fences to defend your base.
